What you can do

Immersive 3D or classic 2D

Choose a realistic 3D chessboard with zoom, tilt, and orbit controls, or a clean 2D board for traditional play.

Hands‑free voice moves

Tap Voice and say moves like “e2 to e4,” “castle king side,” or “cancel.” Built‑in fuzzy matching helps resolve what you meant.

Play vs AI or a friend

Face a range of AI strengths with time limits, or play person‑vs‑person on the same device.

Import from a screenshot

Load a 2D chessboard image, crop to the board, calibrate once, and correct any square — then continue the game from that position.

Save, resume, and replay

Save games to continue later. Premium unlocks automatic replay with play/pause, step, and speed controls to review and improve.

Privacy‑minded

Board recognition and voice processing run on‑device. Network access is used only for App Store purchases.

Quick start

  1. From the main menu, choose New Person vs Person or New vs AI (select a difficulty).
  2. Use the on‑screen controls to undo, save, or toggle voice.
  3. To import a position, open Menu → Load from Image…, crop to the board, calibrate once from a starting position, fix any squares, then load.
  4. Open Settings to switch between 3D and 2D, and to enable voice speak‑back.

Tip: In voice mode, say cancel to clear your selection.

FAQ

How do I restore purchases?

On the subscription screen, tap Restore Purchase. Consumables (one‑time usage credits) aren’t restorable by design, per App Store policy.

What OS versions are supported?

Requires iOS 17 or later, iPadOS 17 or later, and macOS 14 or later.

Does the app send my images or voice to a server?

No. Image recognition and voice parsing run locally on your device. The app only connects to Apple for App Store purchases.

How does voice input work?

Tap Voice to start listening. Speak a square then its destination (e.g., “e2 … e4”), or say the full move in one phrase. Say “castle king side,” “castle queen side,” or “cancel.”

Replay needs Premium?

Replay is part of Premium and includes a free trial. You can still save and resume games without Premium.
